gives Erik a fresh pool to pick the next houses from. None of the 40 is already on the shortlist or built.

Wikimedia Commons, a Pick toggle and a fit rating, like the cards already there. Fit is rated as in spec 016:
★★★ mostly boxes, ★★ needs one new trick, ★ mostly curves or angles.

  limit, with no title lost.

## Implementation notes
- **Changed:** `src/dev/shortlist.ts` (the 40 candidates after the first 41, plus `batches()`), `src/dev/ShortlistPage.tsx`
  (lead photos fetched in batches). `tests/devPages.test.ts` expected exactly 41 and now expects 81. `tests/shortlistMore.test.ts`
  (spec 041) expected exactly 41; it now checks that its 20 sit at positions 21–40.
- **Tests:** `tests/shortlistForty.test.ts` covers A1, A2 and A5. A1 failed first (41 candidates). `npm run check`: 375 tests pass.
- **A bug the 40 exposed (A5):** the page asked Wikipedia for every lead photo in one request. With 73 titles, more than the API's
  limit of 50, the API rejects the whole request, and **every** card lost its lead photo, the original 41 included. The page now
  sends batches of 50. This was a fix to keep existing behavior, not a change to the page, so it isn't out of scope.
- **Commons searches** were narrowed where the plain search found another building: `Jacobs First House`, `Lever House Park Avenue`,
  the Riva San Vitale category for Casa Bianchi, `intitle:` for the Fisher and Walker houses, and `Centre Pompidou Paris facade`.
  The Wikipedia title "Hopkins House" is a disambiguation page, so the card uses "Hopkins House, Hampstead".
- **A4** (Chrome, after the fix): all 81 cards show, and 61 lead with a Wikipedia photo. Three new cards show the image-search link
  instead of a photo:
  - **Moriyama House** has no photos on Commons, as expected.
  - **Walker Guest House:** the page already drops images named like plans, so its floor plan isn't shown (the Photos section
    above expected it to lead with the plan), and Commons has no photos.
  - **Casa Bianchi:** the one photo in its Commons category is smaller than the page's 800 px minimum.

  The rest lead with the right building. Villa Stein's lead is the Vaucresson photo of Le Corbusier's villa, and Casa de Vidro
  leads with its stair, an interior detail.
